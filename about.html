What's Inside

Limey Bastard is a lime-forward blend built to unleash the full power of flavor. Every ingredient is there for a reason.

No online shop — find us in person

We do not offer online shopping at this time. This blend is locally and artisanally made, and only available in person at select Chicago events. Look for the jar and label shown above.

Ingredients

  • Salt

    The foundation. Balances the acids and heat so the burn hits clean instead of muddy.

  • Citric Acid

    Pure sour punch — brightens the lime profile and makes the heat feel sharper on the tongue.

  • Powdered Lime

    Real lime character in dry form. Zesty, tart, and unmistakably limey — it's in the name for a reason.

  • Malic Acid

    Green-apple tang that layers under the citric bite for a longer, more complex sour finish.

  • Lactic Acid

    A subtle fermented roundness that smooths the edge between sour and spice without dulling either.

  • MSG

    Amplifies every flavor in the blend — makes the lime pop and the chilies taste deeper and richer.

  • Korean Chili Powder

    Gochugaru-style heat with a smoky, slightly sweet backbone — the everyday fire in the mix.

  • Thai Bird's Eye Chili

    Fast, fierce heat that lights up fast and fades just enough to let the next bite hurt again.

  • Ghost Pepper

    Bhut jolokia territory — slow-building, face-melting heat for those who asked for more.

  • Scorpion Pepper

    Sting-forward and floral on the nose, vicious on the palate. Not here to be subtle.

  • Carolina Reaper

    Currently the world's hottest pepper. A small amount goes a very, very long way.

  • Cellulose Powder

    Anti-caking agent that keeps the blend free-flowing so every shake delivers the same punishment.
